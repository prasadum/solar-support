Sun angles: elevation, azimuth and zenith

The person stands at the centre of the sky dome. Move the sun and watch how the three angles change.

Elevation (altitude) α
45°
How high the sun is above the horizon. 0° at sunrise/sunset, 90° straight overhead.
Zenith angle θz
45°
Angle between the vertical (straight up) and the sun's rays.
Azimuth γ
135°
Compass direction of the sun, measured along the horizon clockwise from North.
α + θz = 45° + 45° = 90°

Shadow tip: the person's shadow always points opposite to the sun's azimuth, and gets longer as α gets smaller (shadow length = height ÷ tan α).

Real-sun mode uses local solar time (12:00 = sun due south/north). Textbooks such as Duffie & Beckman measure azimuth from South (East −, West +); both values are shown.
