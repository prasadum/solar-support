C-Rating of Battery

Discharge time & current

C-rate tells how fast a battery is emptied

Capacity (Ah) = water in the tank
Current (A) = flow from the tap
1C = a full battery empties in 1 hour
Same 100 Ah battery 4 s = 1 h
Discharge current
I = C × Ah
2C × 100 Ah = 200 A
Discharge time
t = 1 ÷ C
1 ÷ 2C = 0.5 h = 30 min
C-rate
C = I ÷ Ah
50 A ÷ 100 Ah = 0.5C
Higher C → more current, less time
