Solar Energy Systems

Technical Training & Overview

On-Grid System

Features:
  • Connects directly to utility grid
  • No batteries required
  • Net metering enabled
Advantages:
  • Lowest initial cost
  • Highest efficiency
  • Earn credit for excess power
Disadvantages:
  • Shuts down during power cuts
  • No backup power

Off-Grid System

Features:
  • Independent from utility grid
  • Requires large battery bank
  • Includes charge controller
Advantages:
  • 100% self-sufficient
  • Immune to grid blackouts
  • Ideal for remote areas
Disadvantages:
  • Highest initial cost (batteries)
  • Batteries need replacement
  • Wasted excess power in summer

Hybrid System

Features:
  • Combines grid & battery storage
  • Smart energy management
  • Dedicated critical load port
Advantages:
  • Uninterrupted power supply
  • Export excess to grid
  • Maximize self-consumption
Disadvantages:
  • Complex installation & wiring
  • High upfront cost

Islanding & Anti-Islanding

DANGER: Islanding

Inverter continues to power the local grid during a blackout. This poses a fatal shock hazard to utility workers fixing the lines.

SAFE: Anti-Islanding

Mandatory safety feature (IEC 62116). Inverter senses grid failure and automatically cuts off output in milliseconds.

BESS & IEC Standards

Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)

Crucial IEC Standards

IEC 61215 / 61730

Design qualification and safety approval for Solar PV Modules.

IEC 62109 - 1 & 2

Safety of power converters (Inverters) for use in PV power systems.

IEC 61727 & 62116

Grid interface requirements and Anti-Islanding testing procedures.

IEC 62619

Safety requirements for secondary lithium cells and batteries (BESS).
